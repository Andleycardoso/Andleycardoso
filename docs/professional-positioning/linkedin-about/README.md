## Decision record

The central narrative is not a career break between business and data.

It is a progression:

**Business with data → specialized Data & Analytics practice → Data Engineering & AI as expanded technical capacity.**

The timeline is intentionally expressed as:

- about **9 years** connecting processes, indicators, analysis and decision;
- **5 years in industry**, working inside business areas where data and indicators already supported operational understanding and decisions;
- the **last 4 years** with deeper, specialized professional practice in Data & Analytics;
- current expansion into Data Engineering & AI.

This avoids two distortions: claiming nine years of specialized Data & Analytics experience, or implying that data only became part of the professional journey four years ago.

## Positioning principle

LinkedIn communicates professional identity and positioning. GitHub provides technical evidence and execution history. DataPulseNext expands that thinking into methods, content, experimentation, products and applied solutions.

Minor copy edits do not require a new major version. A major version should represent a material change in professional positioning or evidence.
